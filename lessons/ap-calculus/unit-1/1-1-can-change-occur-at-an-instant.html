Lesson roadmap

Learning outcomes

By the end of the lesson, you will be able to

  • Interpret rate of change at an instant using average rates over intervals containing that instant.
  • Explain why calculus uses limits to understand dynamic change.
  • Explain why average rate of change is undefined at one point.
  • Use the limit idea to define instantaneous rate of change.

Warm-up: average speed or instantaneous speed?

Think - pair - share

  1. A car traveled 180 km in 3 hours.
  2. The speedometer reads 72 km/h right now.
  3. A student grew 6 cm from age 10 to age 12.
  4. The population is increasing at this exact moment.

Classify each as average or instantaneous. Explain your reasoning.
