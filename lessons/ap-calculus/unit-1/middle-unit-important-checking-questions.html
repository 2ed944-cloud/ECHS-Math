Reference

Course connections

This checkpoint revisits change and instantaneous motion (Topic 1.1), limit notation (1.2), graphical limits (1.3), numerical limits (1.4), and continuity and equivalent-expression reasoning used in Lessons 1.5–1.6. It is a checkpoint placed after Lesson 1.6, not an additional numbered AP topic.

The questions, models, tables, graphs, and solutions are original classroom practice. They are not official AP exam questions.
