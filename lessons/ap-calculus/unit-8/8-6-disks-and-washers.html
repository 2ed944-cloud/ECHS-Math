Volumes by Disk and Washer Methods

Revolving a slice perpendicular to the axis makes a disk or a washer. A hole is removed using π(R² − r²).

Revolving the region x² ≤ y ≤ x, 0 ≤ x ≤ 1 about the x-axis gives V = π∫₀¹(x² − x⁴) dx = 2π/15. About the y-axis, horizontal slices give V = π∫₀¹(y − y²) dy = π/6.

Volumes have cubic units. A finite sum approximates the solid; the integral gives its exact volume. The lesson's model controls, worked examples and questions appear when JavaScript is available.
