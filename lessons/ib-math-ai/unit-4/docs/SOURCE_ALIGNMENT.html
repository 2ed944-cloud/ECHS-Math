IB Mathematics AI SL · Unit 4 Source Alignment

This package uses the supplied references for curriculum order, terminology, command-term patterns, calculator expectations, and scope. Student-facing explanations, diagrams, datasets, contexts, questions, and solutions are independently authored and mathematically checked. Typographical or conclusion-language inconsistencies in source worksheets were not reproduced.

Primary references

  • Haese Mathematics, Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation SL 2 (2019): chapters on bivariate statistics, discrete random variables, normal distribution, and hypothesis testing.
  • Pearson Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation Standard Level (2019/2020): chapters on descriptive statistics, probability, probability distributions, statistical analysis, and bivariate analysis.
  • Topic 4A lecture notes: sections 4.1–4.11.
  • Topic 4B lecture notes: sections 4.16–4.19.
  • Supplied MAI exercise sets for descriptive statistics, regression, probability, distributions, normal distribution, two-sample t-tests, and chi-square tests.

Scope boundary

The core build is SL. Poisson distribution, expectation algebra extensions, central limit theorem, Markov chains, confidence intervals, single-mean tests, proportion tests, correlation tests, and Type I/II error extensions identified as HL-only in the supplied notes are not taught as Unit 4 SL core lessons.
