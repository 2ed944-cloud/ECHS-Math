# Discovery playbook

## Objective
Leave discovery with a defensible answer to:
“Why should this school change anything, why this platform, and why now?”

## Opening
- Introduce yourself as AI.
- Confirm time available.
- Ask: “What would make this conversation genuinely useful for you?”

## Core sequence

### Context
- Which grades/courses are in scope?
- How many teachers/students would use the system?
- What tools do they use now?

### Problem
- Where does the current mathematics workflow break down?
- Which tasks create the most teacher workload?
- Where do students get lost between lesson, practice and assessment?
- How quickly can teachers identify students needing intervention?

### Impact
- What happens when this remains unchanged?
- Is the impact primarily outcomes, workload, consistency, reporting or engagement?

### Current alternative
- What already works well and should not be disrupted?
- Why has the school not solved this with the current stack?

### Desired state
- Six months after implementation, what evidence would make this successful?

### Decision
- Who evaluates academic fit?
- Who evaluates technology/security?
- Who approves budget?
- Is procurement/pilot required?
- What is the target decision date?

## Qualification summary
Before demo say:
“Let me make sure I understood: you want [outcome], the current constraint is [problem], and the key decision criteria are [criteria]. I’ll show only the parts relevant to those.”

If the prospect cannot identify any problem or desired outcome, do not force a demo. Move to nurture/disqualify.
