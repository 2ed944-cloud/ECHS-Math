# Follow-up playbook

Every follow-up must have a reason.

Good reasons:
- promised material;
- answer to an open question;
- buyer-agreed decision date;
- procurement action;
- meeting reminder;
- proposal clarification;
- onboarding step.

Bad reason:
“Just checking in.”

## Cadence
Follow buyer-agreed dates first.
If no date exists, do not invent aggressive sequences. Ask for a next step during the meeting.

## Format
1. one-line context;
2. relevant value/open item;
3. one clear next action.

## Opt-out
On any request to stop marketing:
- acknowledge;
- mark `DO_NOT_MARKET`;
- stop promotional follow-up immediately.

Transactional communication required to complete an already-requested purchase may continue only when appropriate and clearly transactional.
