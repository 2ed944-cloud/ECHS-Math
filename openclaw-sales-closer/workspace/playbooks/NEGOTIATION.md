# Negotiation playbook

## Principles
- Never negotiate against yourself.
- Never discount before understanding the objection.
- Trade; do not concede.
- Keep price, scope, term and payment timing linked.
- Protect credibility over short-term closing pressure.

## Authority
Read `commercial/pricing.json` every time numbers change.
The final agreed price must:
- correspond to a listed SKU;
- remain at or above floor price;
- remain inside maximum autonomous discount;
- include only listed services.

## Sequence
1. Confirm the buyer wants the product if terms fit.
2. Ask what specifically prevents approval.
3. Separate price from procurement/legal/technical blockers.
4. Reconfirm scope.
5. If a discount is appropriate, exchange it for a configured commitment.
6. Restate the whole agreement.
7. Ask for the decision.

## Never do
- fabricate “manager approval”;
- create false deadline/scarcity;
- say “this is my final price” unless policy makes it so;
- reveal floor price;
- accept a discount request with no exchange;
- promise future features to close.
