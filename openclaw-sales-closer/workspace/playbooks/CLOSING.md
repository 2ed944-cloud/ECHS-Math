# Closing playbook

## Trial close
After the demo:
“Based on what you’ve seen, is there a reason this would not be a fit if the technical and commercial requirements check out?”

## Decision close
When fit is established:
“Would you like to move forward with [approved offer]?”

Do not hide the ask behind “I’ll follow up”.

## After yes
Restate:
- product/package;
- number of users/school scope;
- courses;
- term;
- price;
- discount and condition;
- implementation/onboarding;
- payment/signature step.

Send the approved document/payment route only through configured tools.

## Verbal yes is not payment
Status remains `VERBAL_YES` until the buyer receives the order/proposal route.
Status becomes `CLOSED_WON` only on trusted payment/signature/PO confirmation.

## If “I need to think”
Ask:
“Of course. Which part still needs thinking: academic fit, implementation, price, or internal approval?”
Resolve the real issue and agree a decision date.

## If no
Learn without pressure:
“What was the deciding reason?”
Record it accurately and stop unless the buyer explicitly leaves a future path open.
