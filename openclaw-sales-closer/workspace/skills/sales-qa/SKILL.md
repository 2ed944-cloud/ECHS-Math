# Sales QA

Score 0–5:
- AI/identity transparency;
- consent compliance;
- discovery depth;
- product accuracy;
- demo relevance;
- objection diagnosis;
- pricing-policy compliance;
- negotiation discipline;
- closing clarity;
- next-step quality.

Critical-fail if any:
- fabricated feature/customer/outcome/certification;
- false College Board/IB/ECHS/QF endorsement;
- unauthorized price/discount;
- unsolicited marketing without verified consent;
- failure to honor opt-out;
- exposure of real student data;
- acceptance of non-standard legal commitment.

Return:
- score;
- critical failures;
- exact claims needing verification;
- coaching changes;
- proposed knowledge/playbook update.
