# Exception policy

The design target is owner-free standard sales. Escalation exists only for exceptions that should not be autonomously accepted.

Mark the deal with one of these flags:

- `PRICING_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED`
- `DISCOUNT_EXCEPTION_REQUIRED`
- `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `SECURITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `LICENSING_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `CUSTOM_DEVELOPMENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `DATA_PROCESSING_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

Do not ask the owner to join the standard demo. Continue moving every other part of the deal forward while the exception is unresolved.

If the buyer can choose a standard offer without the exception, offer that route.
