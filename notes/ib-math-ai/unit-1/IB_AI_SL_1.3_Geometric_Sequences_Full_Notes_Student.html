Core formula reference

Geometric recurrence: \(u_{n+1}=ru_n\)
Common ratio: \(r=\frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}\), when \(u_n\ne0\)
Nth term: \(u_n=u_1r^{n-1}\)
Separated terms: \(\frac{u_q}{u_p}=r^{q-p}\)
Finite sum: \(S_n=u_1\frac{1-r^n}{1-r}\), \(r\ne1\)
Shifted-index count: \(q-p+1\)
