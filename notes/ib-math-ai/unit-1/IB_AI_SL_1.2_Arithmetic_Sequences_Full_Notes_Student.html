Core formula reference

Arithmetic structure: \(u_{n+1}-u_n=d\)
Nth term: \(u_n=u_1+(n-1)d\)
Finite sum: \(S_n=\frac n2(u_1+u_n)\)
Equivalent form: \(S_n=\frac n2[2u_1+(n-1)d]\)
Recover a term: \(u_n=S_n-S_{n-1}\)
Shifted-index count: \(n-m+1\)
