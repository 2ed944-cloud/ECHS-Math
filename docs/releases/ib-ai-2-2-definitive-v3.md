# IB Mathematics AI SL · Lesson 2.2 Definitive Release 3.1.0

## Scope

Lesson 2.2 has been rebuilt as a five-block, classroom-ready lesson on linear and quadratic models.

- 72 required Learn screens; 80 with enrichment.
- 72 required Practice questions; 80 with enrichment.
- 12 required Timed Quiz questions; 14 with enrichment.
- 3 required IB tasks; 4 with enrichment.
- 20 Practice questions at each level: Foundation, Application, Reasoning and Challenge.

## Teaching blocks

1. Linear Models: Rate, Intercept and Equation.
2. Line Relationships, Intersections and Constraints.
3. Quadratic Structure and Features.
4. Solving, Intersections and TI-84 Evidence.
5. Model Choice, Validation and IB Synthesis.

## Calculator decision

TI-84 Plus CE training is included because graph windows, zeros, extrema, intersections and whole-number threshold tables are relevant to this lesson and to Paper 2 practice. The calculator never replaces the manual mathematical structure.

The concise guided workflows are:

- Zero.
- Maximum.
- Intersect.
- TABLE with adjacent admissible values.

The third-party simulator is lazy-loaded beside the active slide so the teacher can keep the mathematical example visible while students follow on physical TI-84 calculators.
