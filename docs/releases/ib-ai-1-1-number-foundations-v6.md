## Scope

The lesson now follows one coherent path:

1. number sets;
2. decimal places;
3. significant figures;
4. rounding;
5. exact values, approximations and estimates;
6. direct and calculated bounds;
7. absolute, relative and percentage error;
8. normalized scientific notation and orders of magnitude.
