## Interactive behavior

Objective questions accept a best-choice response or exact numeric/fraction input, with distinct DNE, undefined, and signed-infinity answers. Hints and attempt-gated worked solutions support recovery. First-attempt correctness without hints is reported separately from later corrections. Written responses have editable drafts, explicit three-point-per-part classroom rubrics, and self-assessment; they are not automatically awarded official AP scores. Responses can be downloaded.

Graphs include scales, open/filled markers, descriptions and numerical alternatives. Sliders and selectors support keyboard use. Edited answers invalidate stale feedback; edited written work clears its old self-score. Saved work is scoped to the current ECHS account on the device; when storage is unavailable, students receive a clear message. The protected platform continuation remains in charge of course progression.
