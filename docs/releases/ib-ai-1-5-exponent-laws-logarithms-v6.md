# IB AI SL Lesson 1.5 — Exponent Laws and Logarithms v6.0.0

## Scope

Lesson 1.5 is rebuilt as a complete multi-day pathway rather than a short logarithm introduction. The instructional sequence is:

1. meaning of an exponent and exact-power structure;
2. product, quotient and power laws;
3. zero and negative exponents;
4. rational exponents, exact values and real-domain restrictions;
5. common-base exponential equations;
6. inconsistent and identity equations;
7. exponential substitution;
8. logarithms as inverse exponents;
9. common and natural logarithms and change of base;
10. logarithm laws, conditions and misconception repair;
11. exponential growth and decay inversion;
12. continuous versus discrete threshold interpretation;
13. graph-intersection and substitution verification;
14. logarithmic scales, sound intensity and pH-style interpretation;
15. reconstruction and evaluation of exponential models.

## Mathematical coverage

- \(a^ma^n=a^{m+n}\)
- \(\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}\), with \(a\ne0\)
- \((a^m)^n=a^{mn}\)
- \(a^0=1\), with \(a\ne0\)
- \(a^{-n}=\dfrac1{a^n}\)
- \(a^{m/n}=\sqrt[n]{a^m}\), with required real-domain restrictions
- common-base exponential equations
- equations that have no solution or all real solutions
- substitution for expressions quadratic in \(b^x\)
- \(b^y=x\Longleftrightarrow\log_bx=y\)
- \(\log_bx=\dfrac{\log x}{\log b}=\dfrac{\ln x}{\ln b}\)
- product, quotient and power laws for logarithms, with positive arguments
- exponential growth and decay target times
- adjacent-period checks for discrete thresholds
- base-10 logarithmic scale differences and multiplicative ratios
- sound-level and pH-style contexts
- exponential model reconstruction from two observations

## Assessment design

Practice Studio contains 24 questions in each of Foundation, Application, Reasoning and Challenge. Timed Quiz prompts are independent of Practice Studio. The five extended tasks assess:

1. exact exponent structure and reasoning;
2. culture growth and first whole-period thresholds;
3. medicine decay and model interpretation;
4. logarithmic sound intensity;
5. reconstruction and evaluation of an exponential model.

Every task part has an answer, marks and a part-level markscheme, and every task’s part marks reconcile with its stated total.
