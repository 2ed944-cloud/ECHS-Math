# AP Precalculus 1.2 — classroom presentation, revision 4.2.0

The previous release put 43 classroom stops and the remaining practice/reference material into one 82-slide linear deck. Repeated explanations and small question columns slowed live teaching. This revision provides 30 classroom slides, then an explicit choice of further practice. Fifteen superseded slide containers are consolidated into their relevant activity or review; 37 optional practice/reference slides remain separately navigable (67 total).

## What changes for teaching

- The route begins with a warm-up, Activities 1 and 2, then a student turn. Ten activities are numbered consecutively.
- Each major activity opens with explanation, visible key notes and, where applicable, a worked model. The second step opens the exploration. This keeps large text readable without placing all the merged content on screen together.
- All 59 objective questions have a short, task-specific preparation note. Multiple questions on a slide display one at a time. Six written tasks receive a reasoning checklist before their prompts; independent solutions and rubrics remain attempt-gated.
- Teaching text and key notes are 24–28 px on classroom screens, with larger headings, response choices, controls and table text. Mobile uses a single column. Worked teaching examples are visible by default and can be concealed for discussion.
- Classroom Next/End stops at review. Extra practice has its own counter, selector and return button. The long reference checklist remains optional.

The sequence retains all ten supplied ideas, with original ECHS examples and questions. Concavity is explicitly connected to the Topic 1.1 prerequisite; Topic 1.2 does not require derivative notation, formal limits or trigonometric calculation. Pace the chapters across the available teaching sessions; the complete route is not claimed to fit one standard period.
