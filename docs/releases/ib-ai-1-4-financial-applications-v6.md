# IB AI SL Lesson 1.4 — Financial Applications v6.0.0

## Unit architecture

Unit 1 is consolidated into six lessons:

1. Number Foundations, Scientific Notation and Approximation
2. Arithmetic Sequences and Series
3. Geometric Sequences and Series
4. Financial Applications
5. Exponent Laws and Logarithms
6. Technology for Equations and Systems

Approximation, bounds and percentage error remain in Lesson 1.1. Loans, annuities and amortization are merged into Lesson 1.4. Technology for Equations and Systems is renumbered from 1.8 to 1.6. Legacy lesson URLs redirect to the appropriate canonical lesson.

## Instructional sequence

Lesson 1.4 follows one coherent financial-modelling route:

1. cash-flow language, direction and valuation date;
2. simple interest as arithmetic change;
3. compound interest as geometric change;
4. nominal, periodic and effective annual rates;
5. inverse growth questions and completed-period thresholds;
6. reducing-balance depreciation;
7. inflation, nominal value, real value and exact real return;
8. ordinary annuities and annuities due;
9. mixed lump sums and regular deposits;
10. savings targets and withdrawal funds;
11. TVM variables, cash-flow signs and solver evidence;
12. loan repayments and lifetime interest;
13. amortization recurrences and schedules;
14. retrospective and prospective outstanding balances;
15. extra payments, term tradeoffs and final-payment rounding;
16. sensitivity, real-value comparison and financial recommendation.

## Scope boundary

The supplied Haese loans-and-annuities material includes growing annuities. The public lesson provides one final, clearly labelled optional extension screen for this idea. Core Practice Studio, Timed Quiz and IB Tasks assess fixed-payment annuities, loans and amortization rather than growing-annuity formulas.
