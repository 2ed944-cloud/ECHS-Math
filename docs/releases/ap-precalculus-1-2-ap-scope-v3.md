# AP Precalculus 1.2: Rates of Change, release 3.0.0

The lesson now uses the same ECHS slide experience as the revised Topic 1.1, with local KaTeX, exact graph models, interactive answer checking and scored written-response review. The earlier practice studio is replaced by a curated sequence that keeps the focus on Topic 1.2 and distinguishes interval averages from point-rate estimates.

## Curriculum basis

Reviewed against the College Board AP Precalculus Course and Exam Description effective Fall 2026, Topic 1.2 (printed page 31), with the introductory approach to local rates reflected in the pacing:

https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-precalculus-course-and-exam-description.pdf

| Framework | Lesson coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 1.2.A.1 | Output change divided by input change; a constant rate producing the same net endpoint change |
| 1.2.A.2 | Approximate a rate at a point using sufficiently small intervals containing it, when a rate exists |
| 1.2.A.3 | Compare point rates through nearby average-rate estimates |
| 1.2.B.1–3 | Quantities varying together; signs, magnitudes, units and contextual interpretations |
| Skills 2.A and 3.A | Read graphical, numerical, analytical and verbal evidence; match precision to the available information |

The straight endpoint comparison supports the average-rate definition. Systematic linear/quadratic rate patterns and function-family classification remain for Topic 1.3. There are no required derivative rules, formal limit calculations, advanced uncertainty bounds or unrelated challenge topics.

## Delivered lesson

- 55 slides, organized for two teaching sessions with later practice and review.
- Ten investigations: tank endpoints; movable comparison lines; zero average versus constant behavior; unequal intervals; left/right/centered estimates; comparison at two points; equal global averages with different local behavior; display precision; calculator quotients; and a corner investigation in the challenge section.
- Eight formative checks, 24 original AP-style MCQs and four six-point FRQs.
- Six challenge MCQs and two six-point challenge FRQs follow the main AP-style questions. Retained challenge ideas include recovering missing values, solving for a parameter, a symbolic interval average, unequal interval weighting, a jump within a valid whole interval, and the danger of relying on a centered estimate at a corner.
- Eighteen two-point written parts provide 36 possible self-assessed points. These are original classroom scoring guidelines, not official College Board questions or full-course exam simulations.
- Calculator-required tasks identify their status; calculations retain full endpoint values until final rounding.
- Hints, attempt-gated solutions and written scoring guidelines; editing requires renewed checking or rubric review.
- Account- and revision-scoped storage; pending edits are saved before switching accounts. Storage denial is handled without losing the open lesson. Investigations reset on reload.
- Canonical URL retained. Old named slide links map to relevant new content, and the legacy redirect preserves query parameters and the requested section.
- Protected practice continuation delegates to the platform access controls. Self-assessed lesson points do not award platform mastery.
- The portal links directly to revised practice and FRQs. Existing companion PDFs remain clearly labeled separate earlier resources.
