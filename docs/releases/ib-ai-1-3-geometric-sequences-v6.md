# IB AI SL Lesson 1.3 — Geometric Sequences and Series v6.0.0

## Scope

The lesson follows one coherent progression:

1. sequence, ratio, series and partial-sum language;
2. constant-ratio classification and arithmetic-versus-geometric structure;
3. positive, fractional and negative ratios, including sign and magnitude behaviour;
4. recursive, explicit, tabular, graphical and contextual representations;
5. why the explicit rule uses the exponent `n − 1`;
6. distant terms, inverse indices and separated-term parameter recovery;
7. even- and odd-index-gap reasoning and symbolic consecutive terms;
8. growth and decay thresholds with adjacent-stage verification;
9. sigma notation, shifted limits and finite geometric sums;
10. inverse finite-series problems, percentage models, validation and limitations.

## Delivery contract

- 73 purposeful Learn screens
- 96 Practice Studio questions, balanced 24 per level
- 14 independent Timed Quiz questions
- 5 IB-style extended-response tasks

- geometric-sequence explorer linking first term, common ratio, generated terms, ratios, graph, behaviour and finite sum;

## Mathematical coverage

- `u_{n+1}=ru_n` and `u_n=u_1r^{n-1}`;
- positive growth, positive decay, alternating growth and alternating decay;
- separated terms through `u_q/u_p=r^{q-p}`;
- sign ambiguity for even index gaps;
- the consecutive-term condition `B^2=AC`;
- finite geometric series and shifted blocks;
- growth and decay factors from repeated percentage change;
- term and cumulative thresholds;
- bouncing-ball and repeated-effect applications;
- residuals, saturation and model evaluation.

## Scope boundary

Haese organizes geometric sequences, growth and decay, finite geometric series and infinite geometric series as distinct parts of the broader sequence-and-series chapter. The supplied MAI 1.6 exercise collection emphasizes nth terms, finite sums, separated terms, inverse problems and threshold confirmation. Accordingly, the public lesson treats finite geometric sequences and series as core and places infinite geometric series on one final screen labelled as optional enrichment. Infinite-series content is excluded from Practice Studio, Timed Quiz and IB Tasks.
