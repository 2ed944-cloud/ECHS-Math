# IB Mathematics AI SL · Lesson 2.2 Definitive Release 5.0.0

## Rebuild scope

Lesson 2.2 has been rebuilt from a blank instructional sequence while preserving the canonical lesson URL and the platform routes.

- 80 purposeful Learn screens.
- 80 original Practice Studio questions: 20 Foundation, 20 Application, 20 Reasoning and 20 Challenge.
- 16 independent Timed Quiz questions.
- 6 original IB-style extended-response tasks with complete answers and markschemes.
- Six classroom teaching blocks covering linear structure, line relationships, quadratic features, construction, solving and model validation.

## Mathematical graphics

All instructional graphs are purpose-built from the equations and coordinates used in the lesson. The release checks roots, intercepts, vertices, axes, intersections, physical domains and residual patterns rather than reusing generic decorative curves.

## TI-84 Plus CE training

The lesson pairs manual mathematics with verified workflows for:

- Zero.
- Minimum and Maximum.
- Intersect, repeated near every relevant crossing.
- TBLSET and TABLE for discrete thresholds.

TI-84 Classroom provides Teacher Demo, Students Follow and Exam Drill modes. The third-party TI-84 practice simulator is lazy-loaded, sandboxed and displayed beside the active lesson slide on desktop, with a full-width mobile layout.

## Quality gates

Dedicated deterministic and Chromium QA validate:

- exact content and assessment counts;
- balanced Practice levels and unique IDs/prompts;
- complete task mark totals and markschemes;
- corrected high-risk numerical values;
- no malformed KaTeX delimiters or raw commands;
- all 80 Learn screens without horizontal overflow, math errors or missing visuals;
- interactive linear, quadratic, difference and residual laboratories;
- Practice, Quiz and IB Tasks routes;
- TI-84 Classroom workflows, simulator docking and mobile behaviour.
