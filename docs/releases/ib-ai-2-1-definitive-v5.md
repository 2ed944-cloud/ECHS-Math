# IB Mathematics AI SL Lesson 2.1 — classroom release 5.0.0

## Purpose

Lesson 2.1 has been rebuilt as the conceptual and technological foundation of Unit 2. The route now develops one major function idea at a time, uses a data-integrity investigation rather than a routine tariff as the opening problem, and separates mathematical understanding from calculator procedure.

## Course alignment

The lesson addresses the current function requirements represented in the supplied curriculum materials:

- SL 2.2 — functions, notation, domain, range and inverse as reflection;
- SL 2.3 — graph of a function;
- SL 2.4 — key graph features and intersections using technology.

The sequencing and terminology were checked against the supplied Haese functions chapter, Pearson material, current syllabus mapping and the available examination-style mappings.

## Six teaching blocks

1. Relations and Functions
2. Function Notation
3. Domain and Range
4. Graph Features
5. TI-84 Evidence
6. Inverse Relations and Synthesis

## Delivery contract

- 88 purposeful Learn screens
- 96 Practice Studio questions: 24 per level
- 18 independent Timed Quiz questions
- 6 original IB-style tasks
- local TI-84 workflow lab for Zero, Minimum/Maximum, Intersect and TABLE/TBLSET

## Instructional changes

- replaces the parking-fee opening with a solar-canopy data audit;
- removes repeated mapping and representation screens;
- distinguishes relation, function, image, preimage, domain, codomain and range precisely;
- strengthens graph reading: intercepts, extrema, sign, direction of change, holes, jumps and asymptotes;
- requires contextual-domain checks after numerical solving;
- teaches calculator evidence as setup, settings, output, verification and interpretation;
- develops inverse relations through coordinate exchange, reflection in y=x and one-to-one reasoning;
- removes development, release and quality-assurance language from the student lesson.

## Technology

The TI-84 lab is local to the lesson and uses no external calculator service. It provides four focused workflows with graph/table displays, key sequences, predicted answers, numerical checking and interpretation prompts.

## Assessment design

Practice and Quiz prompts are independent. The six extended tasks cover relation auditing, graph analysis, contextual domain and range, intersections, model comparison, inverse interpretation and technology-supported synthesis. Every task includes complete marks and markscheme evidence.
