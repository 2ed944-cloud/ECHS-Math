# IB AI SL Lesson 1.2 — Arithmetic Sequences and Series v6.0.0

## Scope

The lesson follows a coherent progression:

1. sequence, term, series and partial-sum language;
2. constant first differences and arithmetic classification;
3. recursive, explicit, tabular, graphical and contextual representations;
4. direct terms, inverse indices, thresholds and parameter recovery;
5. sigma notation, shifted limits and term counting;
6. derivation and use of finite arithmetic-series formulas;
7. cumulative thresholds, positive terms and recovery of terms from partial sums;
8. technology, validation, modelling assumptions and IB-style assessment.

## Delivery contract

- 73 purposeful Learn screens
- 96 Practice Studio questions, balanced 24 per level
- 14 independent Timed Quiz questions
- 5 IB-style extended-response tasks

## Interactive components

- arithmetic-sequence explorer linking first term, common difference, list, first differences, graph and partial sum;
- sigma-notation anatomy and selectable examples;
- generated-question studio for structure, nth terms, sums and inverse problems;
- focused task and part navigation with locally preserved student responses.

## Reference basis

Scope, terminology and task structure were checked against the supplied Pearson sequence/series material, Haese Core Topics SL 1, and Nikolaidis MAI 1.4–1.5 arithmetic-sequence exercises. Explanations, examples, visuals and assessment items are original ECHS adaptations.

## Validation

The release is gated by JavaScript syntax checks, exact content counts, balanced Practice levels, unique IDs and titles, task-mark reconciliation, independent arithmetic spot checks, KaTeX-safe math, self-contained practice prompts, assessment paging/scrolling, portal metadata and guarded-artifact integration.
