# ECHS Commercial Offer Matrix

Use this file as the default packaging logic until owner-approved pricing is added.

## Student
Best for individual AP/IB/advanced mathematics learners.
Value story: structured learning, practice, mastery, mistake recovery, spaced review and assessment.
Commercial model: monthly/term/annual subscription.

## Teacher
Best for individual mathematics teachers or private tutors.
Value story: reusable teaching/practice resources, assignments, class organization and learning evidence.
Commercial model: annual teacher licence.

## Department
Best for a mathematics department running one or more advanced/international programmes.
Value story: consistent practice/assessment workflow across teachers, centralized resources and class-level insight.
Commercial model: annual licence priced by teacher/student band.

## School / Institution
Best for schools needing managed accounts, classes, teachers, students and parent visibility.
Value story: institution-managed mathematics environment with structured access, assignments and progress evidence.
Commercial model: annual institutional licence plus optional onboarding/support.

## Tutoring Centre
Best for centres delivering AP, IB or advanced mathematics to multiple cohorts.
Value story: common resources and practice workflows across instructors and learners.
Commercial model: annual centre licence by active user band.

## Customized Deployment
Best for organizations needing branding/configuration/integration work beyond the standard product.
Commercial model: setup fee + annual licence/support. Scope and legal terms require owner approval.

## Pricing rule
No numeric price is considered approved until recorded explicitly in this file by the owner or an authorized commercial decision. Until then, the agent may research comparable offers and recommend pricing but must label it as proposed.
