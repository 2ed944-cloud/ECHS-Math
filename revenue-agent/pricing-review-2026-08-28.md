The strongest verified response came from **Al Hussan**, a Saudi multi-school operator with active AP mathematics provision.

The initial message combined:
1. specific institutional relevance (multi-school group + AP provision),
2. specific ECHS fit (AP Calculus/AP Precalculus + authenticated teacher private-bank tools),

4. very small starting deployment (1–3 secondary mathematics teachers at one or two campuses),

The prospect then requested detailed information about product capabilities, AP workflows, private-bank tools, implementation references, licensing, technical requirements, onboarding/training, pilot objectives, roles, evaluation criteria, data/feedback, timeline and support. This is the clearest current buying-intent evidence.
