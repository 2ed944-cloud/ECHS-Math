# ECHS Pricing Engine — Provisional Institutional List Prices

Status: internal commercial baseline for testing. Do not publish as a permanent public price list without review of conversion data.

## Market logic
ECHS is sold as a specialized advanced-mathematics learning/practice/assessment platform. Institutional pricing should be simple enough to quote quickly while preserving room for volume, term and strategic-account negotiation.

## Qatar baseline list prices
Use QAR as the reference currency. For other countries, quote in local currency using current FX where practical and preserve approximately equivalent economics.

### Individual Teacher
QAR 900 / year
- One teacher.
- Use only where institutional licensing is not yet practical.
- Does not imply unlimited school-wide student licensing.

### Department Core
QAR 7,500 / year
- Up to 100 students.
- Up to 5 teachers.
- One primary advanced-math pathway/program focus.
- Standard onboarding/support.

### Department Pro — Recommended
QAR 12,500 / year
- Up to 250 students.
- Up to 10 teachers.
- Multiple supported advanced-math courses/pathways.
- Standard onboarding/support.
- Preferred target for established AP/IB mathematics departments.

### School Advanced Mathematics
QAR 22,500 / year
- Up to 500 students.
- Up to 25 teachers.
- Supported AP/IB/advanced mathematics coverage as verified in the production platform.
- School-level rollout and standard onboarding.

### Tutoring / Test-Prep Center
QAR 10,000 / year
- Up to 150 active students.
- Up to 10 instructors.
- Supported advanced-math programs.
- Additional active-student blocks may be quoted separately.

### Multi-Campus / Network / Enterprise
Starting reference: QAR 40,000 / year
- Quote based on campuses, users, programs, onboarding, reporting and customization.
- Use a three-option proposal.

## Pilot pricing
### Standard qualified pilot
Free
- 14-30 days.
- Up to 3 teachers.
- Up to 40 students.
- Defined curriculum/use case and success criteria.

### Extended/large pilot
Reference: QAR 2,500
- For materially larger or longer pilots.
- Credit the pilot fee toward the first annual license when the customer converts within the agreed window.

## Customization / services
Do not bundle material custom development into the base license.
Quote separately for:
- large custom content work,
- bespoke integrations,
- dedicated implementation,
- white-label configuration,
- unusual reporting/workflow requirements.

## Discount authority
Apply COMMERCIAL_AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md:
- Up to 10%: routine close accelerator when justified.
- 10-15%: trade for annual prepay, broader adoption, longer term or meaningful reciprocal value.
- 15-20%: strategic exception only.
- Never below 80% of standard quote without strategic owner decision.

## Term incentives
Preferred:
- 12-month annual license.
- Annual upfront payment.

Possible negotiated incentives:
- 2-year commitment: up to 10% effective concession when strategically justified.
- 3-year commitment: up to 15% effective concession when strategically justified.

Do not automatically combine maximum term incentive with another maximum discount.

## Quote-selection logic
Choose the smallest tier that credibly solves the buyer's stated need, then show an expanded option when there is clear value.

Examples:
- One AP Calculus class: pilot -> Department Core.
- Established AP Calculus + AP Precalculus department: Department Pro.
- Multiple advanced mathematics programs across high school: School Advanced Mathematics.
- Multi-branch tutoring organization: Center or Enterprise.

## Pricing conversation
Do not lead cold outreach with price. First establish fit, then use pricing to make the next decision easy.

## Competitive test
If a serious buyer presents a legitimate competing quote, compare scope and economics rather than reflexively undercutting. The agent may restructure scope/term or use the discount ladder but should protect margin and perceived value.

## Review cadence
After every 10 qualified pricing conversations or 3 closed institutional deals, analyze:
- win rate by tier,
- discount level,
- objections,
- sales-cycle length,
- deployment size,
- willingness to pay,
- churn/renewal indicators.
Then recommend changes to list prices based on evidence.
