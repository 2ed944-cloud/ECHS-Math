Privacy

Two operating modes: the public lesson and Phase 2 learning tools can continue locally in the browser. When the school activates institutional accounts, signed-in learning records are also synchronized to the school’s configured Supabase project so authorised students, teachers, administrators and linked parents can use role-based dashboards.

School-managed account information

The institutional platform may store a username, full name, school email, role, grade, student or staff identifier, class membership, parent–student relationship, account status, last login time and password-update time. Accounts are created by authorised school staff; public self-registration and Google sign-in are not used.

Passwords and sessions

Passwords are never stored as readable text. They are converted to secure password hashes on the server. An initial or reset password is displayed once to the authorised creator and cannot later be retrieved. The system stores hashes of random session tokens, expiry times and limited security metadata used for login protection. Students are not given a password-change or public password-recovery feature; an administrator or authorised teacher resets the password.

Learning information

Learning records may include lesson progress, assignments, question identifiers, responses, correctness, course, unit, topic, practice mode, timestamps, session duration, mastery scores, evidence levels, Mistake Bank and spaced-review records, streaks and assignment results. Teacher dashboards use aggregated and student-level records for instructional support. Parent accounts can access only students explicitly linked to them.

Local browser information

The platform may also store selected courses, bookmarks, completion marks, local practice and test attempts, mastery, review schedules, active sessions, interface preferences and offline files in the browser. Signed-in students can synchronize supported local learning records to their school account. When offline, pending data may remain on the device until connectivity returns.

Access controls

Administrators manage all institutional accounts. Teachers receive the account and class access allowed by school settings and may reset permitted student passwords. Students access only their own learning account. Parents and guardians access only linked students. Sensitive credential and session tables are not exposed directly to the browser.

Account imports and credential files

An administrator or authorised teacher may import account details from CSV. The resulting credential file contains usernames and initial passwords and should be stored, transmitted and deleted according to school security procedures. ECHS Mathematics cannot recover the original password after the one-time credential output.

Service provider and data location

When enabled, institutional data is stored in the Supabase project selected and controlled by the school. Supabase Edge Functions process account and learning requests. The school administrator is responsible for selecting the project region, access permissions, retention period and deletion procedures that meet school policy and applicable law.

External resources

Some lessons link to external video, graphing, document or publisher services. Opening an external service is subject to that provider’s privacy terms. The separate Official AP environment keeps its own source and publication boundary.

Offline storage and the installed application

The installable web application caches public platform files and previously requested learning content. Private institutional API responses are deliberately excluded from service-worker caching. A user can remove locally stored information through browser or application site-data settings.

Managing and deleting data

Local learning history can be reset from the learning tools. Institutional account suspension revokes active sessions. Permanent account, class and learning-data deletion must be completed by an authorised school administrator through the database administration process. Before production launch, the school should publish its retention and deletion schedule and identify a privacy contact.

Deployment notice: institutional accounts remain disabled until the school deploys the supplied backend, approves this privacy model and updates config/institution.json. The interface must not be presented as a live account service before those steps are complete.
